# Research & Publications

## Research Focus Areas

### 1. Quantum Transport in Topological Insulators
We investigate low-temperature electronic transport phenomena in three-dimensional topological insulators. By fabricating ultra-clean nanodevices, we measure edge-state transport and quantum interference patterns under strong magnetic fields.

### 2. 2D van der Waals Heterostructures
Using mechanical exfoliation and dry-transfer techniques, our lab constructs novel stackings of graphene, hexagonal boron nitride (h-BN), and transition metal dichalcogenides (TMDs) to discover correlated electron states.

### 3. Cryogenic Device Engineering
Our experimental techniques rely on dilution refrigerators capable of reaching temperatures down to 10 mK paired with high-frequency microwave filters and low-noise amplifier chains.
