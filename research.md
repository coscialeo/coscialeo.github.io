## Research and publications

### Research focus areas

1. **Anyon superconductivity in moiré crystals.** Experimental evidence shows FCI states and unconventional supercondictivity coexisting in twisted bilayer MoTe<sub>2</sub>. It has been theorized that the latter may be the consequence of pairing between the anyonic excitations of the former, as first proposed, in a context presenting continuous translational symmetry, by Laughlin. What, then, is the effect of a periodic potential on the anyon superconductor phase?
