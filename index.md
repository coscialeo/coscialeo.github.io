## About & Research Overview
I lead the **Quantum Transport & Topological Matter Lab**. Our research focuses on experimental condensed matter physics, specifically exploring quantum coherent transport, novel topological insulators, and 2D heterostructures under extreme conditions (low temperatures and high magnetic fields).

---

## Research Focus Areas

### 1. Topological Quantum Transport
Investigating edge states and Majorana zero modes in hybrid superconductor-semiconductor nanowires for fault-tolerant quantum computing.

### 2. 2D van der Waals Heterostructures
Fabricating ultra-clean twisted bilayer graphene devices to probe strong electron correlations and unconventional superconductivity.

### 3. Ultrafast Terahertz Spectroscopy
Using time-resolved optical pulses to track non-equilibrium phase transitions in correlated electron systems on sub-picosecond timescales.
